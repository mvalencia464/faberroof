## 1. Hero Section (Solving for All 4 Decision-Makers)

> **Goal**: Match visitor expectations instantly, establish the core value proposition, and convert fast-moving decision-makers above the fold.

### 🎯 Primary Headline Formulas (H1)

*Select one based on market sophistication and primary customer trigger:*

- **Formula A (How to + Desirable Outcome + Without Anxiety)**:
  > **"How to Get a Leak-Proof, Insurance-Covered Roof Replacement Without the Contractor Headaches."**
- **Formula B (The "Only" Value Prop)**:
  > **"The Only Spokane Roofing Team That Guarantees 24-Hour Emergency Tarping and Zero Out-of-Pocket Surprise Fees."**
- **Formula C (Direct Response / Problem Match)**:
  > **"Stop Water Damage Before It Wrecks Your Home. Get a Full Roof Inspection in Under 24 Hours."**

---

### 📝 Supporting Subheadline (H2)

> *"You get factory-certified installers, transparent line-item pricing, and full insurance claim support—so your home stays protected for the next 30 years."* *(Applies the "U" Rule and promises an immediate functional outcome).*

---

### 🔘 Hero Call-to-Value (CTV) Buttons

*Joanna’s rule: Replace scary, high-friction "Calls to Action" (like "Submit" or "Contact Us") with "Calls to Value" that complete the subconscious thought: "I want to...".*

- **Primary Button**: `[ Show Me My Free Roof Estimate ]` *(Calls to Value outperform standard action buttons by neutralizing anxiety).*
- **Secondary Button (Low Friction)**: `[ See 10-Minute Storm Damage Checklist ]`
- **Click-Trigger Sub-Text** *(Positioned directly under the primary button to eliminate hesitation):*
  > *"✓ Zero obligation • No credit card required • Licensed & Insured in WA"*

---

## 2. Problem & Agitation Section (PAS Framework)

> **Goal**: Hold up an uncomfortable mirror to the reader’s daily reality using Voice of Customer (VOC) data to create emotional necessity before presenting the solution.

### 📌 Section Crosshead (H2)

> **"Put an End to Leaky Ceilings, Unreliable Contractors, and Surprise Repair Bills."** *(Formula: "Put an end to [Struggle]").*

### 📋 Agitation Copy Blocks (Body Copy)

*(Written strictly with short, certain declarations and audited with the "U" Rule):*

- **The Unexpected Storm**: *"You hear the heavy rain pounding your shingles at 2 AM. By morning, a yellow water stain is spreading across your living room ceiling. You know a minor drip today turns into thousands of dollars in rot and mold tomorrow."*
- **The Contractor Chase**: *"You call three local roofers. Two don't pick up. The third promises a quote by Tuesday, shows up late, and dumps a vague, handwritten estimate in your inbox with no timeline."*
- **The Insurance Nightmare**: *"Your insurance adjuster uses technical jargon to deny half your claim, leaving you stranded between a leaking roof and an out-of-pocket disaster."*

---

## 3. The Solution & Benefit-Feature Stack

> **Goal**: Introduce the solution as a total relief, using Joanna’s strict **"Benefit + *because* + Feature"** bullet formula.

### 📌 Section Crosshead (H2)

> **"This Isn't Just a Patch Job. It's Complete, Weather-Proof Peace of Mind."** *(Formula: "This isn't [Low-Value Category], it's [True Desire]").*

### ⚡ Front-Loaded Bullet List (Competitive & Methodical Decision-Makers)

*(Joanna teaches that logical, fast-moving decision-makers scan for clear benefits front-loaded with features):*

- **You save thousands on emergency repairs** *because* our thermal-imaging cameras detect hidden underlayment leaks before water touches your drywall.
- **You never fight insurance companies alone** *because* our certified claims specialists document every damaged shingle and meet your adjuster on-site.
- **Your home stays spotless during construction** *because* we deploy specialized magnetic nail-sweeps and heavy-duty property shields around your yard daily.
- **You get lifetime structural protection** *because* we install GAF-certified heavy-weather shingle systems backed by a 50-year transferable warranty.

## 4. Proof & Trust Builders (Humanistic Decision-Makers)

> **Goal**: Satisfy slow-moving, emotional decision-makers who need personal stories, real human faces, and undeniable credibility before committing.

### 🏆 The "One Boulder" Proof Section

*(Joanna advises against cluttering the page with meaningless badges; lead with one massive, undeniable proof metric).*

> **"Over 1,400 Spokane Families Protected. Zero Failed Inspections in 15 Years."**

### 💬 Social Proof / Testimonial Block (Metric-Driven)

*(A high-converting testimonial overcomes a specific sales objection and includes tangible numbers).*

> **"They handled my entire insurance claim and had my new roof on in 48 hours."** *"After the hail storm, I was terrified my insurance would deny our claim. The team arrived the next morning, met directly with the adjuster, and got $18,000 in roof damage fully approved. ## 5. Transparent Trade-Offs (Preemptive Disqualification)

> **Goal**: Disarm prospect skepticism by openly stating who you are *not* for. Admitting a limitation builds massive trust for the main claim.

### 📌 Section Crosshead (H2)

> **"We Are Not the Cheapest Roofers in Town—Here's Why That Saves You Money."**

### 📝 Body Copy

> *"If you are looking for a quick, $500 handyman patch job using leftover shingles, we are not the right fit for you. Cheap roofing shortcuts always lead to double the cost when leaks return next winter. We only install complete, code-compliant roofing systems installed by factory-certified crews. You get honest line-item estimates, premium materials, and warranties that actually hold up when you need them."*

## 7. Objection-Crushing FAQs (Methodical Decision-Makers)

> **Goal**: Methodically answer the exact logical questions standing between the prospect and the click.

- **Q: How quickly can you get here if my roof is actively leaking?**
  - **A:** *We dispatch emergency tarping crews within 2 to 4 hours across the Spokane metro area to prevent further interior water damage.*
- **Q: Do I have to pay anything out of pocket if I use insurance?**
  - **A:** *In most cases, your only out-of-pocket cost is your standard insurance deductible. We provide line-item quotes matching insurance payout guidelines so there are no surprise fees.*
- **Q: How long does a full roof replacement take?**
  - **A:** *Most residential roof replacements are completed in just 1 to 2 days, including full tear-off, installation, and magnetic yard cleanup.*
